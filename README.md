## About my work

My projects explore full-stack development, AI-powered media creation, speech translation and workflow automation. I also experiment with game development and tools that make working with coding agents easier.

- **Web & business applications:** dashboards, inventory systems and corporate websites.
- **AI & media:** voice translation, identity-preserving portraits and music video generation.
- **Automation & integrations:** affiliate content workflows, video APIs and agent monitoring.
- **Experiments:** cryptocurrency paper trading and a mobile tower-defense prototype.
